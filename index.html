ABOUT US

Welcome to Hospimedica Group of Companies

The Hospimedica Group was founded in 1989 by Mr. Sunil Anand – a professional with now over 36 years experience in the field of medical equipment and disposables. The Group was founded with vision “To Impact The Healthcare Needs Of Every Indian Family In Some Positive Manner Through Putting Cutting Edge Technologies Into The Hands Of Indian Doctors” and every member are pleased to achieve the vision with the mission “To Achieve Profitable Growth Consistently And With Integrity” .

The Hospimedica Group is engaged in the import, distribution, installation and after-sales service of sophisticated imported medical equipment & also offer Turnkey Solutions.

The Hospimedica Group represents on an exclusive basis over 40 reputed International manufacturers in the fields of Physiotherapy, Rehabilitation, Neurosciences, Urology & Andrology, Critical Care, Operating Rooms, ICU, Disaster Management, Regional Anaesthesia, ENT, Psychiatry, Patient Simulation & Skill Lab etc.

The Hospimedica Group comprises of two companies Hospimedica International Ltd. and Medical Point (I) Ltd. Hospimedica Group has its corporate headquarters in New Delhi and has a country wide network in 11 other cities viz Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bangalore, Kochi, Hyderabad, Coimbatore, Pune, Ahmedabad, Lucknow & Chandigarh to provide effective support to over 8000 installations.

The Hospimedica Group has Far-sighted management with a long term commitment to the business. We have professionally qualified and motivated personnel make Hospimedica the first choice for the medical equipment needs of the customers in the given segments. Hospimedica has set new standards in India and is now one of the leading medical equipment/devices supplier to major Government, Defence and Private Hospitals across the country.

The Hospimedica Family comprises over 140 employees drawn from India’s finest educational Institutions and equipped with intensive classroom, factory and on the job training. Hospimedica employees regularly participate in International training programs to refresh their knowledge and to bring it at par with the advancements in technology.

Regular interaction with customers is maintained through personal visits, participation in conferences, workshops, seminars etc. Information about the products and its applications are also made available through advertisements in professional journals, mailers, newsletters etc. Patient education booklets are also prepared in local languages to help improve patient awareness about diagnostic methods and treatment options available.

We remain committed to provide customer satisfaction to every user in the country.
